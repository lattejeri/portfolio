    body: <h2><strong>Turning a dormant 2018 brochure site into something fitting
      for Energize's next growth step</strong></h2><p>On the ground, Energize
      Industries is a heavyweight commercial electrical contractor. For over 20
      years, they’ve delivered high-voltage upgrades, main switchboards,
      greenfield supermarkets, and critical network shutdowns across Queensland
      for Tier-1 builders and major brands like ADCO, Hutchinson Builders,
      Coles, Woolworths, and Brisbane Racing Club.</p><p>Online, however, you
      wouldn’t have known it. Their legacy website (<code>eesm.com.au</code>)
      has been untouched since before Covid, and did a poor job of demonstrating
      their strongest differentiators. Domestic odd-job services are mixed in
      with high-stakes statutory compliance work, and there was no way offering
      no way for potential clients to download a capability statement or submit
      tender requests.</p>

    body: <h4>Prototype the information architecture</h4><p>Rather than jumping
      straight into high-fidelity visuals or lorem ipsum layouts, I built an
      interactive multi-page HTML schematic prototype, paired with a live
      in-browser copy editor and a master copy deck. This let us stress-test the
      narrative flow, sentence-case editorial standards, and B2B messaging
      across all 9 page templates before locking the visual design.</p>
  - type: text
    body: <h4>Translate electrical trade craft into a sharp schematic visual
      language</h4><p>Moving from prototype to Figma, we steered away from
      generic 'tech startup' bento boxes in favour of a bold, full-bleed
      industrial aesthetic inspired by electrical schematics and site safety
      signage:</p><ul><li><p><strong>Blueprint dot-matrix grids &amp; hairline
      borders:</strong> Architectural <code>1px</code> borders, <code>0px</code>
      corner radii, and corner dot-matrix overlays that echo CAD drawings and
      switchboard schematics.</p></li><li><p><strong>Custom schematic symbol
      library:</strong> A bespoke set of 24px technical icons and electrical
      symbols pairing <code>Geist</code> with <code>Geist Mono</code> for
      specification callouts</p></li><li><p><strong>High-contrast industrial
      palette:</strong> Deep carbon black (<code>#060505</code>), warm paper
      white (<code>#FFF9F7</code>), and high-vis safety orange
      (<code>#FD5404</code>).</p></li></ul><p></p>
