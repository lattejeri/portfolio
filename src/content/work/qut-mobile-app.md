    body: "<h3>How might we maintain customer centricity through product ownership,
      long after a capital project is wrapped up?</h3><p>Capital projects often
      suffer from a predictable drop-off: once development budget expires and
      contingent teams roll off, customer research halts, design files become
      siloed, and product evolution stalls into reactive
      bug-fixing.</p><p>Joining late in the project lifecycle meant navigating a
      brand-new technology stack, stabilising a delivery team affected by key
      departures, and managing stakeholder expectations around reduced capacity
      as we shifted into maintenance mode. Preserving the app’s momentum
      required building an operational ecosystem rather than executing a single
      handoff task.</p>"
